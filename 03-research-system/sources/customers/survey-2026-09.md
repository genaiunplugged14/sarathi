# Customer survey, September 2026

Date: 2026-09-12. Fake data for the Sarathi course.

412 customers answered. The question was: "Would a 60 day return
window change what you buy from us?"

- 38 percent said they would buy more electronics with a 60 day window.
- 9 percent said the return window does not matter to them.
- The top reason given was "I need time to test a monitor or headphones
  at home".
